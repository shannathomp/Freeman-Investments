FREEMAN AIRPORT RIDES

Privacy Policy

Private airport transportation in Atlanta, Georgia

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Freeman Airport Rides, operated by Freeman Investment Corp ("we," "our," or "us"), respects your privacy. This policy describes how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you visit our website, request a quote, reserve transportation, or contact us.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, pickup or destination address, travel date and time, passenger count, reservation details, messages you send us, and payment confirmation information. Our payment processor handles payment card information; we do not need to receive your full card number to process a reservation.

Our website may also collect technical information such as device or browser details, pages visited, and interactions with the site through cookies, analytics, or advertising technologies.

2. How we use information

  • Provide quotes, arrange rides, and manage reservations.
  • Send booking confirmations, service updates, and respond to questions.
  • Process payments and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Maintain safety, comply with legal obligations, and improve our website and services.
  • Measure advertising effectiveness, subject to applicable law and available privacy choices.

3. Information sharing and third-party services

We do not sell or rent customers' personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for those parties' independent marketing purposes.

We may disclose information to trusted service providers only as needed to run our business, including payment processing (such as Stripe), address lookup and mapping (such as Google), website hosting, communications, and advertising measurement (such as Meta Pixel). These providers may process information under their own applicable privacy terms. We may also disclose information if legally required, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a legitimate business transfer.

Our use of Meta Pixel may involve transmitting website interaction data to Meta for measurement and advertising. Please review your cookie and advertising settings and the privacy information provided by those services.

4. SMS and email communications

If you text us or provide your phone number for reservation communications, we may use it to answer your inquiry and send ride-related messages. Mobile opt-in information and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be provided to messaging vendors solely to deliver communications you requested.

Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop nonessential text messages by replying STOP, where supported, or contacting us at the email address below. Reply HELP, where supported, or contact us for assistance. We do not send promotional texts without any consent required by applicable law.

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for reservations, customer support, business records, fraud prevention, and legal compliance, then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

6. Your privacy choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may also manage browser cookies and advertising preferences through your device, browser, or relevant advertising service.

7. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here with a new effective date.
